<!--# Catalog Page -->
<img src="/splunk-show-docs/images/catalog_page.png" alt="my_page" style="width: 40%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 0;">

The Catalog page provides a tabular view of all content that you have access to in Splunk Show.

<img src="/splunk-show-docs/images/catalog_filters.png" alt="my_page" style="width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 0;">

* **Search** field - Simply type key words to quickly and dynamically filter the list.

<figure style="max-width: 80%; margin: 0 0 1rem 0;">
</figure>

* **Type** dropdown - Filter by type of content, such as demos or workshops
  <img src="/splunk-show-docs/images/type_dropdown.png" alt="my_page" style="width: 20%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 0;">

* **Request** dropdown - Filter by the type of request, such as on-demand demos, always on demos (formerly called "static demos") or shared demos.
  <img src="/splunk-show-docs/images/request_dropdown.png" alt="my_page" style="width: 20%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 0;">

* **Updated** dropdown - Use this to filter by content that was recently updated, such as the last 30 days.
  <img src="/splunk-show-docs/images/updated_dropdown.png" alt="my_page" style="width: 20%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 0;">

* **More filters** - Provides an additional set of filters to help you further refine the results.
  <img src="/splunk-show-docs/images/catalog_more_filters.png" alt="my_page" style="width: 50%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 0;">

Click on any row in the table to view a quick summary of the content, with options to **launch an instance**, view **more information** (which takes you to the full template page) or to **copy a link** to that template (good for sharing!)

  </figcaption>

**Not the template you're looking for?** Simply click the row again to minimise it and carry on browsing the catalog!
