<!-- # Signing In -->

Show uses Cisco CCI for sign-in. The steps depend on whether you are a Cisco employee, or a partner or public user.

**Splunkers:**

- On the Show sign-in page, select Sign in with Cisco.
- Enter your @cisco.com email address.
- Follow the prompts to authenticate through Cisco Workforce Duo.

**Partners:**

- [Create a Cisco.com ID](https://id.cisco.com/signin/register) - if you do not already have one.
- On the Show sign-in page, select Sign in with Cisco.
- Enter the email address associated with your Cisco.com ID and follow the sign-in prompts.
- If you do not yet have access to Show please email us at [splunk-show@cisco.com](mailto:splunk-show@cisco.com)

Once you are successfully authenticated, you will be directed back into the Splunk Show UI.

For more information on the cut over from Okta authentication to Cisco authentication please see: [Duo Cutover Instructions](https://splunk.github.io/splunk-show-docs/duo-cutover/).
