<!-- # Signing In -->

Show uses Cisco CCI for sign-in. The steps depend on whether you are a Cisco employee, or a partner or public user.

**Splunkers:**

- On the Show sign-in page, select Sign in with Cisco.
- Enter your @cisco.com email address.
- Follow the prompts to authenticate through Cisco Workforce Duo.

**Partners and Public Users:**

- [Create a Cisco.com ID](https://id.cisco.com/signin/register) - if you do not already have one. If you have previously used Show with the deprecated Splunk.com (Okta) login use the same email address you used to sign in before.
- On the Show sign-in page, select Sign in with Cisco.
- Enter the email address associated with your Cisco.com ID and follow the sign-in prompts.

{{% notice style="info" title="Note About Account Changes" %}}
If you don't have access to the Show templates/view you are expecting through your cisco.com account, email us at [splunk-show@cisco.com](mailto:splunk-show@cisco.com)
We'll let you know once you've been added.
After we confirm, sign out of Show and sign in again with Cisco.
{{% /notice %}}
