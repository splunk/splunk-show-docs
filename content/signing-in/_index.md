<!-- # Signing In -->

Navigate to [https://show.splunk.com](https://show.splunk.com) to get started. You will be redirected automatically to the Splunk website login workflow.

* **Splunkers:** Make sure to use your Splunk Okta credentials, with the @splunk.com extension for proper routing. 
* **Partners:** Use your regular splunk.com login

Once you are successfully authenticated, you will be directed back into the Splunk Show UI.

{{% notice style="info" title="Show Access" %}}

* **Splunkers:** If you receive an error message from Okta that reads **App Access Locked**, you do not have the proper access to access Show and should file a request via our [portal](https://splunk.show/request)
* **Partners:** If you do not yet have access to Show please email us at [splunk-show@cisco.com](mailto:splunk-show@cisco.com)
{{% /notice %}}
