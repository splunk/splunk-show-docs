To manage a workshop event, locate the event on the home page under **My instances** and click on the event.

In addition to the options available for a 'Normal workshop', events have a range of additional options. These are covered across the following pages:

- [Event Settings & Sharing](/workshops/manage-events/event-settings/) - Event link, privacy, thumbnail, notes and schedule
- [Participants & Enrolment](/workshops/manage-events/participants/) - Inviting participants, event managers and passcode enrolment
- [Managing Event Instances](/workshops/manage-events/managing-instances/) - Managing the instances provisioned for your event
